# MezoFlow – Intelligent MUSD Money Management Platform

**Tagline:** Make Your Digital Dollars Work Smarter.

## 1. Introduction – What Is MezoFlow?

MezoFlow is an intelligent decentralized finance (DeFi) platform built on the Mezo ecosystem. It helps users manage, save, and explore opportunities to earn returns on their MUSD holdings through a simple and user-friendly interface.

MUSD is a Bitcoin-backed stablecoin in the Mezo ecosystem. Although users can hold MUSD in their wallets, many may not know how to use it effectively in decentralized finance applications.

MezoFlow aims to solve this problem by providing a single platform where users can manage their MUSD, explore available financial strategies, monitor their funds, and interact with supported DeFi protocols.

Instead of visiting multiple platforms and manually managing their investments, users can use MezoFlow to understand their options and manage their money from one dashboard.

The main goal is to make DeFi more accessible, transparent, and convenient for everyone, especially beginners.

## 2. Problem Statement – What Problem Are We Solving?

Despite the growth of decentralized finance, many users still face difficulties when trying to manage their digital assets.

### Problem 1: MUSD holders do not know how to use their funds effectively

Many users may hold MUSD in their wallets without knowing what opportunities are available to them.

They may want to save their money, explore yield opportunities, or manage their funds more efficiently, but they do not know where to start.

**Our Solution:** MezoFlow provides a unified dashboard where users can explore supported MUSD strategies and manage their funds.

### Problem 2: DeFi is complicated for beginners

Most DeFi platforms contain technical terms and complicated interfaces. Concepts such as APY, liquidity pools, lending, borrowing, and vaults can be difficult for beginners to understand.

**Our Solution:** MezoFlow explains financial strategies in simple language, helping users understand how each strategy works, its potential returns, and its associated risks.

### Problem 3: Managing funds across different platforms is difficult

Users often need to visit multiple DeFi applications to compare opportunities, move their funds, and monitor their investments.

This process takes time and requires technical knowledge.

**Our Solution:** MezoFlow brings supported MUSD management strategies together in one application.

### Problem 4: Lack of transparency and monitoring

Users want to know exactly what is happening with their money.

They need answers to questions such as:
- Where are my funds deposited?
- How much MUSD have I deposited?
- What is the current value of my position?
- How much have I earned or lost?
- What fees have I paid?
- Can I withdraw my funds?

**Our Solution:** MezoFlow provides a transparent dashboard with deposit records, withdrawal records, transaction history, strategy information, and portfolio performance.

### Problem 5: Users have to manage their strategies manually

DeFi opportunities can change over time. Users may need to monitor yields, liquidity, protocol conditions, and their portfolio allocation.

Managing everything manually can be difficult.

**Our Solution:** MezoFlow introduces strategy monitoring, smart notifications, and optional automation. Users can review and approve important actions before funds are moved.

## 3. Our Proposed Solution – How MezoFlow Solves These Problems

MezoFlow acts as a smart financial management layer for MUSD.

The platform allows users to:

1. Connect their crypto wallet.
2. View their MUSD balance.
3. Explore available financial strategies.
4. Compare potential returns and risks.
5. Select a strategy based on their financial goals.
6. Deposit MUSD into supported smart contracts.
7. Monitor their investments.
8. Withdraw their funds according to the strategy's withdrawal conditions.

The platform is designed to make the complete process easier without requiring users to understand the technical complexity of every underlying DeFi protocol.

**Important:** MezoFlow does not guarantee profits or fixed returns. All displayed yields must come from verified sources, and users must be informed about financial and smart contract risks.

## 4. How Does MezoFlow Work? – Complete User Workflow

### Step 1: Connect Wallet

When a user visits MezoFlow, they can connect their crypto wallet, such as MetaMask, using supported wallet connection technology.

After connecting, the platform reads the user's wallet address and retrieves relevant on-chain information.

The user can then view their MUSD balance and portfolio information.

### Step 2: View MUSD Dashboard

After connecting the wallet, users are taken to their personal dashboard.

The dashboard displays:

- Total MUSD balance.
- Amount deposited into strategies.
- Current portfolio value.
- Available balance.
- Total deposits and withdrawals.
- Strategy performance.
- Recent transactions.

This gives users a complete overview of their MUSD holdings.

### Step 3: Explore Financial Strategies

Users can visit the Strategy Marketplace to explore available MUSD strategies.

For example:

**Strategy A – Flexible Savings**

A strategy designed for users who want to deposit MUSD and retain access to their funds according to the vault's withdrawal rules.

**Strategy B – Yield Opportunities**

A strategy that allows users to explore verified DeFi protocols that offer potential returns.

**Strategy C – Balanced Strategy**

A strategy that divides funds between different supported opportunities according to predefined allocation rules.

Each strategy should display its expected or historical performance where reliable data is available, associated risks, fees, withdrawal conditions, and protocol information.

### Step 4: Choose a Strategy

Suppose a user has 1,000 MUSD.

They want to explore a strategy that allocates their funds across different opportunities.

MezoFlow presents the proposed allocation before the user commits any funds.

Example allocation:

- 500 MUSD – Flexible savings strategy.
- 300 MUSD – Supported yield opportunity.
- 200 MUSD – Available balance.

These numbers are an illustrative example, not a promise of returns or a claim that these strategies are currently available.

The user can review the allocation and approve or reject it.

### Step 5: Deposit MUSD

Once the user selects a strategy, they can deposit MUSD through the platform.

The smart contract handles the deposit according to the selected strategy.

The platform records the deposit and updates the user's portfolio information.

The user can verify the transaction on the blockchain.

### Step 6: Monitor Portfolio Performance

After depositing, users can monitor their funds from the dashboard.

They can view:

- Current deposit value.
- Historical portfolio value.
- Strategy performance.
- Deposit and withdrawal history.
- Fees and transactions.
- Available withdrawal options.

### Step 7: Withdraw Funds

Users can request withdrawals from their active strategies.

The withdrawal process depends on the underlying smart contract and protocol conditions.

After a successful withdrawal, the user's MUSD balance and portfolio information are updated.

# 5. Main Features of MezoFlow – Detailed Explanation

## Feature 1: Smart MUSD Vault

The Smart MUSD Vault is one of the main features of MezoFlow.

It allows users to deposit MUSD into a smart contract and manage their deposits through a simple interface.

### How it works:

1. The user connects their wallet.
2. The user selects the MUSD Vault.
3. The user enters the amount of MUSD they want to deposit.
4. The platform displays the transaction details.
5. The user approves the transaction.
6. The smart contract receives the deposit.
7. The user's vault position is recorded.
8. The dashboard displays the updated position.

### What does the user get?

Users can view their deposited amount, vault position, transaction history, and withdrawal options.

The vault can also be designed to issue receipt tokens representing a user's share of the deposited assets, if supported by the vault implementation.

**Why is this useful?**

It provides a simple way for users to manage their MUSD deposits without manually interacting with complicated smart contracts.

## Feature 2: Strategy Marketplace

The Strategy Marketplace allows users to discover and compare supported DeFi opportunities.

Instead of searching across multiple applications, users can explore available strategies in one place.

### What does it display?

Each strategy can include:

- Strategy name.
- Description.
- Supported protocol.
- Estimated APY, if verified.
- Historical performance, if available.
- Minimum deposit.
- Withdrawal conditions.
- Protocol fees.
- Smart contract risk information.
- Liquidity information.

### Example:

A user sees two available strategies:

**Flexible Vault**
- Designed for easier access to deposited funds.
- Returns depend on the underlying implementation.
- Withdrawal conditions are displayed.

**Yield Strategy**
- Uses a supported DeFi integration.
- Potential returns depend on protocol performance.
- Includes protocol and liquidity risks.

The user can compare these opportunities and select the one that suits their goals.

**Why is this useful?**

It makes discovering DeFi opportunities easier and more understandable.

## Feature 3: Smart Auto Allocator

The Smart Auto Allocator is a feature that helps users organize their MUSD according to their financial preferences.

Users can choose a predefined strategy or allocation plan.

### How it works:

1. The user enters the amount of MUSD they want to manage.
2. The user selects a preference, such as conservative, balanced, or growth-oriented.
3. MezoFlow displays a proposed allocation.
4. The user reviews the allocation and risks.
5. The user approves the transactions.
6. The smart contract executes the supported allocation.

### Example:

A user deposits 1,000 MUSD and selects a balanced allocation.

MezoFlow might propose:

- 50% in a flexible vault.
- 30% in a supported yield strategy.
- 20% remaining unallocated.

The user reviews the allocation and approves it.

**Why is this useful?**

It reduces the effort required to organize funds and gives users a clear understanding of where their money is going.

## Feature 4: Risk Dashboard

The Risk Dashboard helps users understand the risks associated with their selected strategies.

Every DeFi opportunity has risks, and users should understand them before depositing money.

### What does it display?

- Smart contract risk.
- Protocol information.
- Liquidity conditions.
- Withdrawal restrictions.
- Historical performance.
- Strategy exposure.
- Potential loss scenarios, where they can be reasonably estimated.

The dashboard can also provide simple explanations of technical risks.

For example:

Instead of displaying only "Liquidity Risk: High", the platform can explain:

"Withdrawals may be delayed if the underlying protocol does not have sufficient available liquidity."

**Why is this useful?**

It helps users make informed decisions instead of choosing strategies based only on advertised returns.

## Feature 5: Smart Alerts and Notifications

MezoFlow can notify users about important changes in their selected strategies.

### Example notifications:

- Your deposit was successful.
- Your withdrawal was completed.
- The strategy's reported APY has changed.
- A protocol has announced an important update.
- Your strategy has reached a configured risk threshold.
- Your available withdrawal liquidity has changed.

Users can customize which alerts they want to receive.

**Why is this useful?**

Users do not have to constantly visit the application to check their investments.

## Feature 6: Portfolio Performance Tracker

The Portfolio Performance Tracker gives users a complete view of how their MUSD positions are performing.

### What does it display?

- Total deposited amount.
- Current portfolio value.
- Historical portfolio value.
- Deposits and withdrawals.
- Realized gains and losses.
- Estimated unrealized gains or losses, where applicable.
- Fees paid.
- Performance over different periods.

The platform can use charts to display portfolio performance over time.

**Why is this useful?**

It helps users understand their financial activity and track their positions in one place.

## Feature 7: Transaction History

Users can access a complete history of their MezoFlow transactions.

### Transaction details include:

- Deposit transactions.
- Withdrawal transactions.
- Strategy allocation transactions.
- Contract interactions.
- Transaction hash.
- Transaction status.
- Timestamp.
- Amount of MUSD involved.

Users can click on a transaction hash to verify it using the relevant blockchain explorer.

**Why is this useful?**

It provides transparency and makes it easier for users to verify their activity on-chain.

## Feature 8: AI Strategy Assistant – Future Feature

MezoFlow can eventually introduce an AI-powered assistant that explains DeFi strategies in simple language.

Users can ask questions such as:

- What can I do with my MUSD?
- Explain this strategy to me.
- What are the risks of this vault?
- Why did my portfolio value change?
- What is APY?
- How does this protocol work?

The assistant can use verified protocol information and on-chain data to generate explanations.

The AI assistant should not independently move user funds or promise investment returns. Financial transactions must remain under user authorization and smart contract controls.

# 7. Technology Stack – How We Will Build MezoFlow

### Frontend

**Next.js and React**

Used to build the application interface, dashboard, strategy marketplace, and user experience.

**Tailwind CSS**

Used to create a responsive and modern design.

### Wallet Integration

**Wagmi and Viem**

Used to connect wallets, read blockchain information, and interact with smart contracts.

### Smart Contracts

**Solidity**

Used to develop the MUSD vault, deposit and withdrawal functionality, user position accounting, and supported strategy execution.

### Blockchain

**Mezo Network**

Mezo is the primary blockchain ecosystem for the project.

The platform will integrate MUSD and use Mezo-compatible smart contracts.

### Backend

**Node.js**

Used for application services, strategy data processing, and notifications.

**Supabase**

Can be used to store user preferences, notification settings, and indexed application information.

On-chain financial information should be verified against blockchain data rather than relying only on the backend database.

### Data and Analytics

Blockchain RPC services, supported protocol APIs, and an indexing service can be used to retrieve relevant transaction and protocol information.

# 8. Smart Contract Architecture

MezoFlow can be organized into several smart contract components.

### 1. MUSD Vault Contract

Handles:
- MUSD deposits.
- MUSD withdrawals.
- User deposit accounting.
- Share accounting, if receipt tokens are implemented.
- Vault balance tracking.

### 2. Strategy Manager Contract

Handles:
- Supported strategy registration.
- Strategy selection.
- Allocation rules.
- Strategy execution.
- Strategy permissions.

### 3. Fee Manager

Handles any clearly disclosed platform fees, if implemented.

### 4. Access Control and Security

Handles:
- Authorized contract operations.
- Administrative permissions.
- Emergency pause functionality.
- Protected strategy integrations.

All contracts should be tested carefully before deployment. External protocol integrations should only be enabled after verifying their contract addresses, interfaces, liquidity, and security assumptions.
